# rangin: mechanical

- [Blender files](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-designs)
- variants
    - arzhang
        - height: 25 cm wheels + 25 cm + 90 cm TVs = 140 cm
        - same parts as [arzhang](../arzhang/design/mechanical)
    - 90
        - height: 25 cm wheels + 45 cm + 50 cm TVs = 120 cm
        - TVs are 2.5 deg tilted.
        - parts
            - metal profile: 2 x 20 mm x 20 mm x 1.5 mm x 6000 mm
                - parts:
                    - 4 x 900 mm
                    - 7 x 220 mm
                    - 12 x 80 mm
                    - total: 6100 mm (6.1 m)
                - cut into 4 x (900 mm + 2 x 220 mm + 80 mm = 1420 mm) + (8 x 80 mm = 640 mm) ~= 1500 mm
            - plexiglass
                - 260 mm x 900 mm x 2 mm
            - thin metal sheet
                - 260 mm x 900 mm x 0.5 mm
                - 2 x 120 mm x 900 mm x 0.5
    - 90c: 90 with curved front and back. ⭐️
