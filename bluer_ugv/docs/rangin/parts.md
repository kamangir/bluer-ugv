# rangin: parts

[swallow design](../swallow/digital/design/parts.md) parts + ⬇️. 

1. [AC to DC power adapter](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/power-adapter.md): 12V DC, 1 A.
1. [AC volt meter](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/ac-volt-meter.md): 220 V, 5 A.
1. [DC circuit breaker](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/dc-circuit-breaker.md): 40 A.
1. [DC volt meter](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/dc-volt-meter.md): 0 - 50 V, 0 - 50 A.
1. [Rechargeable sealed lead acid battery](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/SLA-Battery.md): 12 V, 74 Ah x hours of operation.
1. [TV bracket](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/TV-bracket.md): 2 x.
1. [TV](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/TV.md): 2 x 40 inch.
1. [emergency stop switch](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/emergency-stop.md): 100 A DC or relay-controlled.
1. [miniature circuit breaker (MCB)](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/mcb.md): 2 - 3 A @ 220 V AC.
1. [power inverter, 12 VDC -> 220 VAC.](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/power-inverter.md): continuous output ≥ 400-500 W, surge output ≥ 800 W.
1. [relay](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/relay.md): 12 V DC, 25 A.
1. [safety fuse](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-sbc/tree/main/bluer_sbc/docs/parts/safety-fuse.md): 30 mA trip.
