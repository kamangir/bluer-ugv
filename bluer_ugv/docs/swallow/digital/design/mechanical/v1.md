# swallow: digital: design: mechanical: v1

- [Blender files](https://github.com/kamangir/designs)
- material: 20 mm x 20 mm x 1.5 mm x 6000 mm
- parts:
    - 4 x 1000 mm
    - 9 x 360 mm
    - 4 x 120 mm
    - 10 x 80 mm
    - total: 8520 mm (8.5 m)
    - cut into 4 x (1000 mm + 360 mm = 1360 mm) + 3 x (360 mm + 120 mm + 3 x 80 mm = 720 mm) + (2 x 360 mm + 120 mm + 80 mm = 920 mm)
