# swallow: digital: design: mechanical

- [Blender files](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-designs)
- material: 20 mm x 20 mm x 1.5 mm x 6000 mm x 2
- parts:
    - 4 x 1000 mm
    - 10 x 360 mm
    - 16 x 80 mm
    - total: 8520 mm (8.5 m)
    - cut into 4 x (1000 mm + 360 mm = 1360 mm) + 3 x (360 mm + 4 x 80 mm = 680 mm) + (3 x 360 mm + 4 x 80 mm = 1400 mm) ~= 1500 mm
