# bluer_swallow: digital: design: parts

1. 12 VDC -> 5 VDC, 4A, [XL4015](https://www.handsontec.com/dataspecs/module/XL4015-5A-PS.pdf).
1. 12 VDC motors, 20-45 W, 9000 RPM: 2 x rear + steering.
1. 43 A H-Bridge Motor Driver, [BTS7960](https://www.handsontec.com/dataspecs/module/BTS7960%20Motor%20Driver.pdf): rear drive (2 motors) & steering drive (1 motor).
1. Battery: Rechargeable sealed lead acid, 12 V, 7 Ah.
1. Capacitor, 470 μF to 1000 μF, 16 V or 25 V, Electrolytic, 105 °C rated if possible..
1. LED, ~2 V forward voltage, 10-20 mA.
1. Polyfuse, 1.1 A hold, 2.2 A trip, 16 V, resettable, through-hole, e.g., MF-R110.
1. Raspberry Pi 3B+.
1. Resistor, 330-470 Ω, 1/4 watt, 5% tolerance.
1. TVS diode, unidirectional, 600 W, 6.8 V clamp, e.g. P6KE6.8A, DO-15 package.
