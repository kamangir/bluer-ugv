# bluer-swallow: digital: design: mechanical

- [Blender files](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-ugv-mechanical-design)
- material: 20 mm x 20 mm x 1.5 mm x 6000 mm
- parts:
    - 4 x 1000 mm
    - 9 x 360 mm
    - 14 x 80 mm
    - total: 8520 mm (8.5 m)
    - cut into 4 x (1000 mm + 360 mm = 1360 mm) + 3 x (360 mm + 4 x 80 mm = 680 mm) + (2 x 360 mm + 2 x 80 mm = 880 mm)
