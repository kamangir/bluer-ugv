# bluer_sparrow: digital: design: specs

- dimensions: 300 mm x 600 mm
- speed: 10 km / h (max)
- weight: ~15 kg
- wheel diameter: 20 cm (270 RPM) - 30 cm (180 RPM) 
- motor + gearbox: metal, 90-degree, 180 - 270 RPM, 12 V, 10 kg cm torque.

- 270 RPM ~= 10 km / h
