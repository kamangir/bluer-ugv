# bluer-sparrow: design: mechanical

- [Blender files](https://github.com/kamangir/bluer-ugv-mechanical-design)
- metal profile: 20 mm x 20 mm x 1.5 mm x 6000 mm
    - parts:
        - 4 x 600 mm
        - 7 x 220 mm
        - 12 x 80 mm
        - total: 5060 mm (5.06 m)
    - cut into 4 x (600 mm + 2 x 220 mm + 3 x 80 mm = 1280 mm) ~= 1500 mm
- plexiglass
    - 260 mm x 170 mm x 2 mm  
    - 260 mm x 430 mm x 2 mm
- thin metal sheet: 260 mm x 600 mm x 0.5 mm
